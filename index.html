How your privacy works

Your data is encrypted and stays on your device

Your passphrase scrambles your diary entries before they're saved. Without it, no one can read them — not someone borrowing your computer, not your browser, not us. There is no server, no account, no name, no email, and no data ever sent over the internet.

Sharing with your therapist

When you share, you pick a share passphrase — it doesn't have to be the same as your diary passphrase. Give it to your therapist separately (in person, text, etc.). The link you send them is scrambled — it only becomes readable when they enter the passphrase. No one in between can read it.

If you forget your passphrase

That diary is unrecoverable. There's no "forgot password" option — this is the price of not trusting a server with your information. You can start a new diary alongside it, but the old entries are inaccessible forever. Write your passphrase down somewhere safe.

If you clear your browser data

Every diary on this device is deleted permanently. Same reason — there's no backup anywhere.

What this doesn't protect against

Someone looking at your screen while the diary is open. A weak passphrase that's easy to guess. Malware on your device. Your browser history will show that you visited this site (though the diary content itself is encrypted).

If your browser syncs data across devices, the encrypted diary may appear on other devices signed into the same profile. The contents are still encrypted.

When you share

Sharing creates a short link stored temporarily on our server. The encrypted data is stored until the link expires (you choose how long, from 1 day to 3 months), then permanently deleted. The server cannot read your entries without the share passphrase. The full-length link (available when you share) works forever without any server.

Technical details

AES-256-GCM encryption. Key derived via PBKDF2 with 200,000 iterations and a random 128-bit salt. Each save uses a fresh 96-bit IV. Share links are self-contained: salt + IV + ciphertext in base64url. No cookies, no analytics. Sharing sends encrypted data to our relay server, stored temporarily until the link expires. All cryptography uses the browser's native Web Crypto API.
